# TimelyCapsule — Identity Guideline

> Implementation reference for the brand mark. Concise by design: everything
> here is a rule someone can check in review.
>
> Assets in this folder are generated by `design/.build-brand.mjs` from a
> single geometry definition. **Edit the generator, never the output.**

---

## 1 · The mark

**The Transit.** A marker approaching a meridian that has a place reserved for
it.

Two elements and one relationship. The meridian is broken by a **notch**, and the
notch is exactly the diameter of the marker — the marker is the size of the gap
waiting for it. That rhyme is the whole idea and must survive any future
adjustment.

It says: *something is on its way to an appointed moment, and the moment is
already set aside for it.*

### Geometry — 64-unit grid

| | Primary (≥20px) | Minimal (<20px) |
|---|---|---|
| Meridian x | 45 | 46 |
| Stroke width | 5 | 9 |
| Upper segment | 6 → 27 | 6 → 27 |
| **Notch** | **27 → 37 (10u)** | **27 → 37 (10u)** |
| Lower segment | 37 → 58 | 37 → 58 |
| Marker | `r 5` @ `cx 22` | `r 8` @ `cx 19` |

**Segment-to-notch ratio is 2.1 : 1.** This is the number that makes the mark
work. Shorten the segments and the two halves read as a colon; lengthen them and
the notch disappears. Both failures were tested. Do not change it.

**Switch to Minimal below 20px.** The primary stroke thins into invisibility and
the notch closes up.

## 2 · Colour

> **The mark is achromatic. Permanently.**

`05` reserves brass for *waiting and nothing else*. A logo is the most-repeated
element in any identity, so brass in the mark would destroy that reservation
faster than anything else could.

| Context | Mark | Ground |
|---|---|---|
| Instrument register | `#E6E8EC` | `#0B0E14` |
| Record register | `#16181A` | `#E9EAE6` |
| Print, one colour | `#000000` | paper |
| Reversed | `#FFFFFF` | any ≥4.5:1 ground |

Never: brass, gradients, two-tone, drop shadows, glows, outlines-plus-fills.

---

## 3 · Clear space and minimum size

**Clear space: 1 notch-height (10 units) on all four sides.** Measured from the
mark's bounding box, not from the meridian. Nothing enters that space — not the
wordmark, not a rule, not a page edge.

| Application | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Screen, standalone | 16px |
| Screen, in a lockup | 20px |
| Print | 6mm tall |
| Emboss / deboss | 10mm tall |

**Physical production:** increase stroke weight ~15%. A 0.4mm stroke fills with
ink on uncoated stock and disappears entirely in a blind emboss. *Untested on a
real press — proof before any print run.*

---

## 4 · Lockups

**Horizontal** is primary. Mark at cap height of the wordmark; **gap = 1.2× the
mark's width**, which is wider than optical default. This is deliberate: at a
normal gap the mark reads as punctuation attached to the word rather than as a
mark.

**Vertical** for square spaces. Mark centred above, gap = 0.6× mark height.

**Wordmark alone** is permitted wherever the mark has already appeared on the
same surface.

> **The wordmark and both lockups are outlined.** They contain zero live `<text>`
> and no longer depend on IBM Plex Sans being installed anywhere. The contours are
> extracted by `design/.build-wordmark.py`, shaped with HarfBuzz so the kerning
> matches what a browser rendered from the former live-text version — this family
> carries its kerning in GPOS only, so an advance-width walk would silently drop
> it. The outlines are cached in `design/.wordmark-outlines.json`; re-run that
> script only if the letterforms, weights or tracking change.

Wordmark setting: IBM Plex Sans · *Timely* 600 · *Capsule* 400 at 62% opacity ·
tracking −0.2.

**Both lockups are built from the two rules above rather than from fitted
numbers.** An earlier build shipped a mark at 1.55× cap height with a gap of 1.85×
the mark width — neither was the rule. *Known tension:* at cap height the mark's
stroke measures 67 units against the wordmark's 197-unit stem, so the mark reads
about 2.9× lighter than the word beside it. That is the guideline faithfully
executed, and it is the number to revisit if the mark ever reads as punctuation.
Amend this section rather than deviating from it silently.

## 5 · The mark and the motif

These are different things and must not be conflated.

| | The Mark | The Motif |
|---|---|---|
| What | The Transit, as specified here | The Transit geometry, at scale, in-product |
| Where | Favicon, app icon, lockups, print, partner use | Waiting screen, countdown axis, reveal |
| Colour | Achromatic, always | May use brass — it is showing *waiting* |
| Animates | **Never** | Yes — it is an instrument |

The motif is the mark's geometry doing a job inside the product. Applied-identity
testing showed that when the two become visually identical, the mark stops
reading as an identity and starts reading as a UI control. **Keep them at
different scales and never place them on the same surface at the same size.**

---

## 6 · Never

- Never animate the mark. Institution is static (`05`).
- Never rotate, skew, or mirror it. The marker approaches from the left.
- Never close the notch. It is the mark.
- Never place the marker touching or crossing the meridian — that is arrival, and
  the brand lives in the years before.
- Never enclose it in a badge, circle or shield.
- Never re-space the marker. Its distance is 16.5 units and it means *half-way*.
- Never use it as a bullet, a divider, or a UI control.
- Never redraw it at a new size — scale the SVG, or switch density.

```
  social/avatar-{paper,ink}.svg
  social/avatar-{paper,ink}-{1024,400}.png   opaque, square
  CONTACT-SHEET.png        every asset at true rendered size
```

**Web:** serve `favicon.svg` first, then `favicon.ico`, then the PNGs; `icon-192`
and `icon-512` are the PWA manifest sizes, `icon-maskable-*` carry
`purpose: "maskable"`; `icon-180` is `apple-touch-icon`.

**Social:** use the **paper** avatar. Tested on both platform grounds — the
instrument tile is nearly indistinguishable from `#000000`, so on X in dark mode
it loses its edge entirely and the mark appears to float. Paper holds on white
and on black. Avatars are square and opaque: every platform applies its own
circular mask, and a baked-in radius or an alpha channel gives it something to
composite wrongly.

**Avatar sizing is derived, not chosen.** The mark sits at 64% of the tile —
20.5px at a 32px platform render, which clears the 20px density threshold in §3
by half a pixel. Above that the diagonal clear space fails; below it the mark
must switch density. Do not adjust it without redoing both calculations.

> **`design/.build-brand.mjs` regenerates everything in this folder.** It deletes
> only files with generated extensions — this document lives here and an earlier
> version of the script destroyed it on every run. Do not widen that back to a
> blanket delete.

## 8 · Open

**The wordmark commits to the name.** `09 §XIV.1` records "TimelyCapsule" as the
largest unresolved brand risk — a SaaS name on a horological identity. The mark
itself is rename-proof and can ship today. The wordmark and lockups should be
treated as provisional until the name is settled.

**Untested:** physical emboss weight (§3), and the mark's reading in RTL contexts,
where a left-approaching marker may read as departing. Neither blocks the screen
rebuild.
