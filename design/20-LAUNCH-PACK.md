# TimelyCapsule — Public Identity, Version 1

> **Launch pack.** Social identity, profile assets, first twelve posts, screenshot
> direction, hero artwork, motion, a 30-day calendar, website assets, photography
> direction, and a production checklist.
>
> **Inherits, does not invent.** Every decision here traces to `04-VISUAL-IDENTITY.md`,
> `05-IDENTITY-DECISION.md`, `07-EXPERIENCE-BIBLE.md`, `19-T21-MARKETING-REVIEW.md`
> and `brand/IDENTITY.md`. No new colour, no new typeface, no new mark, no new motion.
>
> **Status:** proposal. Nothing registered, nothing published, no images generated.

---

## Part 0 · The governing constraints

Six rules from the constitution bind this pack harder than they bind the product,
because social media is where identities go to be diluted. They are listed first so
every later section can be checked against them in review.

| # | Rule | Source | What it forbids here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No exclamation marks. No emoji.** Product, notification, email **and marketing.** Absolute. | `07 §VIII.5` | Every convention of the medium. This is the single cheapest recognisability win available and it is free. |
| 2 | **Third person for the institution. Never "we".** | `07 §VIII.6` | "We built…", "We're excited…", "Our mission". The bio cannot say "we". This is what makes it read as an institution. |
| 3 | **Never manufacture urgency.** No countdowns on anything but the user's own clock. | `04 §12.3` | "Launching in 3 days", "Spots limited", "Don't miss". A launch campaign that cannot use a launch countdown is the whole difficulty of this pack. |
| 4 | **Behave, don't depict.** No hourglasses, envelopes, wax seals, vaults, telescopes, domes, star fields. No photography of people. | `04 §9`, `07 §II.3` | ~90% of what a stock library returns for "time capsule". |
| 5 | **Never display capsule content anywhere the user didn't put it.** | `04 §12.12` | Real letters in screenshots. Every letter shown publicly is a declared specimen, and is labelled as one. |
| 6 | **The mark never animates.** The *motif* may. | `brand/IDENTITY.md §5–6` | Logo stings, logo reveals, logo loaders. |

And one more, added for this phase:

> **7 · No testimonials, no social proof, no "trusted by".** `07 §X.5` — nothing has
> been kept yet. Saying otherwise on day one poisons the only asset the brand has.

### The strategic problem, stated plainly

A pre-launch social account normally runs on hype, countdown and access scarcity.
All three are permanently forfeited (`01 §VII`). What remains is the only thing this
brand was ever going to be able to trade on:

> **Publish the evidence of care, not the promise of a product.**

The account is a **public workbook of an institution being built to last** — its
geometry, its rules, its refusals, its numbers. A stranger should be able to scroll
it and conclude *these people have thought about this* (`19 §8`) without ever being
told what the product costs.

**One sentence for the whole account:**

> *It should read like the notice board of a standards body that happens to be
> beautiful.*

## Part 1 · Social identity

### 1.1 Display name

**`TimelyCapsule`** — one word, no tagline appended, no emoji, no city, no "| Ship
your memories". Set exactly as the wordmark sets it: *Timely* is the weighted word,
*Capsule* rides at 62% opacity in the lockup. Plain text cannot carry that, so plain
text simply carries the name.

Never: `TimelyCapsule — Letters to the future`, `TimelyCapsule 🕰️`, `TimelyCapsule
(YC W27)`, `TimelyCapsule is coming`.

### 1.2 Instagram bio

Instagram allows 150 characters. Institutions use fewer than they are given.

**Primary:**

```
Letters, sealed until a date you choose, and kept until then.
Encrypted on your device. Not readable here.
Opening 2026.
```

*(118 characters.)*

Three lines, three jobs: what it is, the thing no competitor will write down, when.
No verb addressed to the reader. No "we". No punctuation the voice doesn't own.

**Alternate — shorter, colder, more institutional:**

```
An institution that keeps time.
Records sealed on your device, delivered on the date you set.
Opening 2026.
```

**Alternate — the promise stated as arithmetic:**

```
Sealed on your device. Delivered on the date you set.
Contents unreadable to anyone else, including this account.
Opening 2026.
```

**Rejected, on the record so it isn't quietly revisited:** anything containing
*memories*, *magic*, *journey*, *unlock*, *your future self awaits*, an emoji, or an
exclamation mark. Also rejected: "Coming soon" — it is the vocabulary of scarcity and
says nothing a date doesn't say better.

### 1.3 X (Twitter) bio

X allows 160. Same content, tightened; X reads as a single paragraph, so it is
written as one.

**Primary:**

```
Letters sealed until a date you choose, and kept until then. Encrypted on the device that wrote them — unreadable here. Opening 2026.
```

*(133 characters.)*

**Alternate — the workbook framing, for an account that will post craft:**

```
Building an institution that keeps time. Sealed records, delivered on the date set. The design decisions are published as they are made. Opening 2026.
```

**Location field:** leave empty, or the legal entity's jurisdiction only. Never "The
Internet", never "Everywhere", never a timezone joke.

### 1.4 Link placeholder

Until the product is public, the link resolves to a **single institutional page**,
not a link-in-bio aggregator. A link tree is four competing CTAs; this brand has one.

- **Href:** `timelycapsule.com/opening`
- **Displayed as:** `timelycapsule.com/opening`  — never a shortener, never a UTM
  visible to a human. Shorteners are a permanence liability: the whole brand argues
  it will resolve in 2041, and `bit.ly` is a promise someone else has to keep.
- **The page:** record register. Bounded record on the instrument ground. One
  paragraph, one date, one field for an email address, and the survival section from
  `19 §7` in full. No hero video, no feature grid, no "join 4,000 others" counter
  (that is manufactured urgency wearing a number).
- **The field's label:** `Email address` — and the button says **`Keep me informed`**,
  not `Join the waitlist`. A waitlist implies scarcity; being informed implies a
  register.
- **Confirmation copy, verbatim:** `Recorded. You will hear once, when it opens.`
  Nothing else. That sentence is also the retention policy, and it must be true.

### 1.5 Username

**Preferred:** `@timelycapsule` on every platform, identical. Handle parity across
platforms is itself an institutional signal — mismatched handles read as a brand that
was assembled in a hurry.

If `@timelycapsule` is taken, the fallback rule is:

> **Extend with an institutional suffix. Never abbreviate, never punctuate for
> availability, never add a year, never add "app", "HQ", "io", "get" or "try".**

Ranked fallbacks:

| # | Handle | Why it holds |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `@timelycapsuleco` | "Co." is the oldest institutional suffix there is. Reads as a company of record, not a startup. |
| 2 | `@timelycapsule.co` | Instagram only (dots permitted). Cleanest of the punctuated options because the dot reads as a domain, not as a workaround. |
| 3 | `@timelycapsuleoffice` | Strongest institutional read of the set. Long, but the voice can carry long. |
| 4 | `@keptbytimelycapsule` | Puts the brand's one word first. Best if the name itself later changes — `@kept` survives a rename. |
| 5 | `@timelycapsulerecords` | Accurate to the vocabulary (`Record` is the product noun). Slight risk of reading as a music label. |

**Never:** `@timelycapsule_`, `@timely_capsule`, `@timelycapsuleapp`,
`@gettimelycapsule`, `@timelycapsule2026`, `@thetimelycapsule`, `@tc_official`.

> ### ⚠ One flag before any handle is registered
>
> `brand/IDENTITY.md §8` records **the name as the largest unresolved brand risk** —
> "a SaaS name on a horological identity" — and states the wordmark and lockups are
> **provisional until the name is settled**. The mark is rename-proof; the handles are
> not.
>
> Registering handles is the most irreversible act in this pack. It hardens the name
> in public before that decision has been made. Two options, and this is a founder
> call, not a design one:
>
> 1. **Register defensively, publish nothing.** Claim `@timelycapsule` on all
>    platforms now to prevent squatting, and hold the accounts dark until the name is
>    confirmed. Costs nothing, forecloses nothing.
> 2. **Settle the name first, then register and launch together.** Slower, cleaner.
>
> Option 1 is the recommendation. It is also worth claiming `@kept…` variants at the
> same time, because if the name ever moves, that is the direction it moves in.

## Part 2 · Profile assets

**Rule for this entire part:** the mark is achromatic, permanently
(`brand/IDENTITY.md §2`). Brass appears in profile assets **nowhere** — brass means
*waiting* and a profile picture is not waiting. Any asset below that shows brass is
showing a product artifact inside a frame, not the identity itself.

### 2.1 Profile photo

**Concept — The Transit, at plate scale.**

Not a logo shrunk into a circle. A **struck plate**: the mark deboss-struck into a
square field, cropped by the platform's circular mask so that the meridian runs
edge-to-edge through the crop and the notch sits dead centre. The gap is what a viewer
sees at 32px in a timeline, and the gap is the mark.

Two variants, and the choice is a permanent one:

| Variant | Ground | Mark | Reads as |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Record** *(recommended)* | `#E9EAE6` paper | `#16181A` | An institution's seal. Stands out in a feed of dark-mode logos precisely because everything else is dark. |
| **B — Instrument** | `#0B0E14` | `#E6E8EC` | An instrument face. More striking at large sizes, more generic at 32px. |

Recommend **A**. The feed is the one place the brand competes for attention it hasn't
earned, and paper is the rarer object there.

**Critical spec — density switch.** A profile photo is rendered at 32px on X and 32–56px
on Instagram. That is below the 20px threshold in `brand/IDENTITY.md §3`, measured on
the mark's own height within the tile. Compute the rendered mark height at the smallest
platform size; **if it falls below 20px, ship `mark-minimal.svg`, not `mark.svg`.** The
primary stroke thins to invisibility and the notch closes. This is the single most
likely production error in this entire pack.

**Construction, not generation.** This asset is **vector, exported from
`design/brand/`**. It is not generated. Do not send the mark to an image model — the
2.1:1 segment-to-notch ratio will not survive, and the ratio is the mark.

- Source: `app-icon-paper.svg` (mark on paper, 12.5% safe area)
- Clear space: 1 notch-height (10 units) — but note the circular crop eats the corners,
  so verify clear space against the **inscribed circle**, not the square.
- Export: 1024×1024 PNG, sRGB, no alpha (platforms composite alpha unpredictably).

**`A01`** — see checklist. *Vector export. Not generated.*

### 2.2 Instagram highlight covers

Six. Each is the **same plate** as the profile photo with one engraved glyph
substituted for the mark — a set of instrument markings, not icons.

Construction rule (`04 §5`): uniform stroke, true circles and straight lines, **square
terminals and mitred joins**, on the same 64-unit grid as the mark. Round caps are
forbidden; they read consumer.

| Cover | Glyph | Contains |
|---|---|---|
| **Kept** | The meridian with a marker held at half-distance (the Transit, unmodified) | The brand word. Philosophy posts. |
| **Geometry** | Two horizontal graduations of unequal length | Mark construction, grid, ratio decisions. |
| **Register** | A filled square adjacent to an outlined square, sharing an edge | Dark/paper, the two-register system. |
| **Clock** | Two vertical strokes of unequal height on a common baseline | Dual Clock, durations, the numeral set. |
| **Record** | A rectangle with one corner rule inset | Letters, exports, keepsakes, print. |
| **Rules** | A single vertical stroke, ungraduated | The constitution. What the product refuses to do. |

**Do not label the covers with text.** Instagram renders highlight titles beneath the
circle already; a word inside the circle *and* beneath it is redundancy, and the glyph
set is the point. Titles are set in the platform as lowercase single words: `kept`,
`geometry`, `register`, `clock`, `record`, `rules`.

**`A02–A07`** — *Vector, drawn to the icon spec. Not generated.*

### 2.3 X / Twitter header

**1500 × 500. The hardest asset in the pack**, because X crops it unpredictably on
mobile and overlays the avatar at lower-left.

**Concept — The Meridian, at architectural scale.**

The header is not a banner. It is **one line**: a single vertical meridian, `#16181A`,
positioned at the header's optical third from the right, running full bleed top to
bottom, broken by one notch at the vertical centre. To the left of it, at half the
distance, a single marker disc. Everything else is paper `#E9EAE6` and empty.

This is signature device 5 (`07 §III.5`) doing the one job a header can do: prove that
the brand will spend 1500 × 500 pixels on almost nothing. Negative space *is* the
luxury signal (`04 §2.4`), and no competitor's header will be 96% empty.

**Safe-area spec — non-negotiable:**
- Avatar overlay occupies the lower-left ~180 × 180 with a further ~24px margin. The
  marker disc must sit **right of x=320** so the avatar never touches it.
- Mobile crops to roughly the centre 3:1. Both the meridian and the marker must fall
  inside the centre 1125px, or the header becomes a blank rectangle on phones.
- Because the meridian is full-bleed vertical, it survives every vertical crop by
  construction. That is why it is vertical.

**No wordmark in the header.** The display name sits directly above it in the platform's
own type. Setting the wordmark again is the same word twice in 200 vertical pixels.

**`A08`** — *Vector. Not generated.*

### 2.4 LinkedIn banner

**1584 × 396.** LinkedIn is where the survival question gets asked (`19 §7`), so this
is the one profile surface permitted a sentence.

**Concept — The plate with a caption.**

Same meridian construction as the X header, on paper. In the left third, set in Source
Serif 4 at ~34px, ink `#16181A`:

> **Sealed on the device that wrote it. Delivered on the date set.**

Beneath it, IBM Plex Mono, uppercase, tracking 0.14em, muted `#5E635C`, ~13px:

> `ZERO-ACCESS BY CONSTRUCTION · OPENING 2026`

Nothing else. No tagline, no URL, no icon row, no "Follow us for updates".

**Profile-photo overlay:** LinkedIn places the avatar at lower-left over ~152px. Keep
all type above y=210 and right of x=200.

**`A09`** — *Vector + live type. Not generated.*

## Part 3 · The launch feed — first twelve posts

Ordering matters. Read top to bottom, the twelve are a **sequence, not a grid**: the
mark, then the system, then the instruments, then the refusals, then the date. A
visitor arriving at post 12 and scrolling up should experience it as an argument.

**Format conventions for all twelve:**
- Instagram: 4:5 (1080 × 1350) for single posts, 1:1 (1080 × 1080) for carousels.
- X: 16:9 (1600 × 900) — X crops 4:5 badly in-timeline.
- Every caption below is final copy. It has been checked against `07 §VIII`: no
  exclamation marks, no emoji, no "we", no "just", literal absolute dates.
- Hashtags: **maximum two, lowercase, on their own line, separated from the caption by
  a blank line.** Zero hashtags on X — X hashtags read as 2014. Instagram takes two.
  There is no branded campaign hashtag; a campaign hashtag is a request for
  participation the brand has not earned.

---

### Post 01 · **The mark**

- **Objective** — Establish the mark before establishing the product. An institution
  is recognised before it is understood.
- **Category** — Brand moment.
- **Visual concept** — The Transit at architectural scale on paper, deboss-struck.
  The meridian runs off the top and bottom of the frame; the notch sits at the optical
  centre; the marker holds at half-distance to the left. Raking light from upper-left so
  the deboss reads as depth, not as printed ink. The frame is 85% empty.
- **Image prompt** — see `P01`.
- **Caption**

  > A vertical reference, broken once.
  >
  > The break is exactly the width of the marker approaching it. The gap is not a gap —
  > it is a place that has been kept.
  >
  > The marker never arrives in the mark. It is held at half distance, permanently. The
  > brand lives in the years before arrival, so the mark does too.

- **Carousel** — none. The first post should be one object.
- **Hashtags** — `#kept`

---

### Post 02 · **Two registers**

- **Objective** — Introduce the system rule that governs everything downstream, on day
  two, before anyone has seen a screen. Publishing the rule first makes every later post
  legible.
- **Category** — Design decision.
- **Visual concept** — A single 1:1 frame split by one hairline: left half instrument
  `#0B0E14`, right half paper `#E9EAE6`. No gradient, no crossfade, no soft edge — the
  registers never overlap (`06 §Constraint 2`), and the graphic must obey the same rule
  the motion does. A bounded paper record sits on the dark half, casting one contact
  shadow; the paper half holds nothing.
- **Image prompt** — see `P02`.
- **Caption**

  > One rule governs the colour and the typography at once:
  >
  > Instruments are dark and set in sans. Records are paper and set in serif.
  >
  > A dial is read in a dark room. A letter is read on paper. The interface tells you
  > which one you are looking at before you have read a word of it, and it never
  > crossfades between them — the instrument clears, the frame holds empty, and the
  > record arrives.
  >
  > Two designers given only that sentence would produce interfaces that agree.

- **Carousel** — 4 slides, 1:1
  1. The split frame (as above).
  2. Instrument register: the verified token column — `#0B0E14 ground · #E6E8EC ink ·
     #E0B84A brass · IBM Plex Sans`. Swatches as small squares on a strict grid, hex
     values in Plex Mono.
  3. Record register: `#E9EAE6 ground · #16181A ink · #3F6154 verdigris · Source Serif 4`.
  4. Paper only: *"A record is an object, never a background."*
- **Hashtags** — `#designsystem`

### Post 03 · **The Dual Clock**

- **Objective** — Show the component that carries more brand than the logo (`07 §III.2`).
  This is the product's actual face.
- **Category** — Product · screenshot.
- **Visual concept** — Real captured UI. The Dual Clock alone, cropped out of the
  capsule detail screen, at large scale on the instrument ground with the surrounding
  chrome cropped away. `TRAVELLED 2 y 11 m` / `REMAINING 12 y 1 m`, tabular numerals
  holding their columns. Nothing else in frame but the hairline rule above it.
- **Image prompt** — none. **`S01` — real screenshot capture.**
- **Caption**

  > Almost nothing in software shows you what has already passed.
  >
  > Elapsed and remaining, weighted equally, always together. A progress bar tells you
  > how much is left and implies the rest was waiting. This says how far it has come.
  >
  > It converts a countdown into a journey, and it is on every screen, which is why it
  > carries more of this brand than the mark does.

- **Carousel** — 3 slides
  1. The Dual Clock at 2 y 11 m / 12 y 1 m.
  2. The same clock at 0 d / 14 y 0 m — the day it was sealed.
  3. The same clock at 13 y 11 m / 1 m — the month before it opens. Temperature ramp
     visibly warmed. Same component, three readings, identical position on the frame.
- **Hashtags** — `#kept`

### Post 04 · **A decade is longer than it sounds**

- **Objective** — Make a stranger *feel* a duration rather than read about one. The one
  permitted spectacle (`07 §X.2`).
- **Category** — Time · philosophy.
- **Visual concept** — A single ruled axis running the full width of a 16:9 frame,
  graduated at each year, ten graduations. One marker sits between the first and second.
  Beneath the axis, in Plex Mono, ten dates — 2026 through 2036. The frame is otherwise
  empty. This is the temperature ramp's axis rendered as a printed scale, not as UI.
- **Image prompt** — see `P03`.
- **Caption**

  > Ten years is 3,653 days. A person can hold about three of them in mind at once.
  >
  > That gap — between the number and the felt length — is the reason a letter arriving
  > in 2036 will land differently than one arriving on Friday. Nothing about the letter
  > changes. The distance does the work.
  >
  > Choose the date first. The writing is easier once the distance is real.

- **Carousel** — none.
- **Hashtags** — none.

---

### Post 05 · **The held beat**

- **Objective** — Publish the signature interaction. The most ownable two seconds the
  product has (`19 §4`).
- **Category** — Behind the scenes · motion.
- **Format** — **Reel / video**, 1:1 or 4:5, 6 seconds, silent. See Part 6, `M01`.
- **Visual concept** — Screen recording of the real seal ceremony on the instrument
  register. Approach at constant velocity → **400ms of absolute stillness** → instant
  state change to `Sealed`. Nothing else on screen. No caption burn-in, no music.
- **Caption**

  > The three parts, in order: an approach at constant velocity, a full stop of 400
  > milliseconds, and then an instant state change.
  >
  > Every animation library wants to peak in the middle. Stopping instead is what turns
  > a transition into a threshold — the stillness is the part that tells you something
  > is about to become irreversible.
  >
  > The stop is preserved when a device requests reduced motion. The silence is timing,
  > not motion, so it survives. That version is arguably the purer one.

- **Hashtags** — `#motiondesign`

### Post 06 · **The hallmark**

- **Objective** — Turn a support string into an identity element (`04 §10.3`). A craft
  post for the design audience, and a trust signal for everyone else.
- **Category** — Typography · detail.
- **Visual concept** — `TC-84KZQM3P` set very large in IBM Plex Mono, uppercase,
  tracking 0.14em, ink on paper, filling the width of a 4:5 frame with generous margins
  above and below. Struck rather than typeset: subtle ink spread at the stroke ends, the
  way a serial number is stamped into a movement. One hairline rule beneath it.
- **Image prompt** — see `P04`.
- **Caption**

  > Every instrument carries its maker's mark and its serial number. Every capsule
  > carries one too.
  >
  > It appears in the same monospaced treatment on every screen the record touches: the
  > timeline, the letter, the receipt, the export, the email. Quote it to support and it
  > identifies the record without revealing anything about it.
  >
  > It is derived, not sequential. It does not say how many have been sealed, because
  > that number is nobody's business and the count is not the achievement.

- **Carousel** — 3 slides: the hallmark alone · the hallmark in situ on a timeline row ·
  the hallmark on a printed export, photographed.
- **Hashtags** — `#typography`

### Post 07 · **What it will not do**

- **Objective** — The refusals. This is the post that will travel furthest, and it is
  the most honest thing the account can publish before there is a product.
- **Category** — Philosophy · behind the scenes.
- **Visual concept** — A ruled list on paper, set in Source Serif 4, each line preceded
  by a short em-rule rather than a bullet. Reads as a notice, not as a graphic. No
  strikethroughs, no red, no "❌" — the constitution bans emoji and bans state-by-colour.
- **Image prompt** — see `P05`.
- **Caption**

  > A partial list of things this product will never have.
  >
  > No streaks. No badges. No "on this day". No preview of anything sealed, anywhere,
  > including internally. No progress bar. No confetti. No urgency of any kind — the
  > only clock is the one you set. No sound at a reveal, because a reveal might happen in
  > a hospital ward and there is no way to know.
  >
  > A list of refusals is a more reliable description of a product than a list of
  > features, because features arrive and refusals have to be held.

- **Carousel** — 6 slides, one refusal per slide, each set alone on paper with the
  reason beneath it in muted type. Slide 6: *"Fashion is a trust liability. If it would
  embarrass us in 2035, it fails now."*
- **Hashtags** — none.

### Post 08 · **Kept**

- **Objective** — Plant the one word (`02 §VI`). The single most important post in the
  twelve.
- **Category** — Brand moment · typography.
- **Visual concept** — The word `Kept` alone, Source Serif 4, ink on paper, set at
  roughly one-third of the frame width, positioned on the lower-left third rather than
  centred — centre-alignment is reserved for genuinely symmetrical moments (`04 §8`).
  Deboss-struck into heavy uncoated stock. Raking light. Nothing else.
- **Image prompt** — see `P06`.
- **Caption**

  > One word carries the whole thing, and English gives it away for free.
  >
  > Kept time — the horological term. A chronometer keeps time.
  > Kept safe — stewardship. The institution keeps the record.
  > Kept its word — the promise, and the reason any of the rest matters.
  > A keepsake — what the recipient is left holding.
  >
  > Four meanings, one word, no strain.

- **Carousel** — none. This one is a single object or it is nothing.
- **Hashtags** — `#kept`

### Post 09 · **The waiting screen**

- **Objective** — Answer the acceptance test in public: *does a sealed capsule sitting
  untouched for three years look like something valuable is happening?* (`03 §VII`).
- **Category** — Product · screenshot.
- **Visual concept** — Real captured UI. The full capsule detail screen at rest, dark
  instrument register, in a plain device frame. Hallmark, title, transit axis with the
  marker at a third, Dual Clock, sealed-until date. **No hover state, no cursor, no
  notification, no badge.** The screen's own emptiness is the argument.
- **Image prompt** — none. **`S02` — real screenshot capture.**
- **Caption**

  > This is what a decade of custody looks like on a Tuesday.
  >
  > Nothing is happening, and that is the design brief. A sealed record does not need
  > tending, does not decay if ignored, and produces no notifications — four sends in
  > ten years, none of which is a feature.
  >
  > The test this screen has to pass: if a capsule sitting untouched for three years
  > reads as empty rather than as kept, the design has failed. It is checked at every
  > review.

- **Carousel** — 2 slides: the screen at three years elapsed · the same screen at eleven
  years elapsed, temperature ramp warmed, marker near the meridian.
- **Hashtags** — none.

### Post 10 · **The duration stamp**

- **Objective** — Show the artifact designed to leave the product (`07 §III.4`), and
  demonstrate zero-access without using the word "encryption".
- **Category** — Product · brand moment.
- **Visual concept** — A physical scene. A small printed card, letterpress, resting on
  a bare desk, bearing only `TRAVELLED 9 YEARS, 4 MONTHS, 2 DAYS` in Plex Mono. Single
  contact shadow. Nothing else on the desk. Shot square from above.
- **Image prompt** — see `P07`.
- **Caption**

  > Franked at the moment of opening, never during the wait.
  >
  > It is the only object here designed to be screenshotted, and it is content-blind by

  >
  > A postmark for a distance measured in years.

- **Carousel** — 3 slides: the printed card · the stamp on screen at the end of a reveal
  · three stamps at three durations — `6 DAYS`, `1 YEAR, 0 MONTHS, 0 DAYS`, `23 YEARS, 11
  MONTHS, 30 DAYS` — proving the component holds every magnitude.

### Post 11 · **The ratio**

- **Objective** — Publish the working. The post that earns the design audience, and the
  proof that the mark was reasoned rather than styled.
- **Category** — Design decision · typography.
- **Visual concept** — A construction drawing: the mark on its 64-unit grid, thin

## Part 4 · App screenshots

**Governing rules for every screenshot:**

1. **Real UI only.** Captured from the running app, never re-drawn, never mocked in
   Figma. `19 §3` makes the marketing site depend on the shipped components precisely so
   it cannot drift; screenshots inherit that discipline.
2. **Specimen data only, and consistent.** One fictional set, used everywhere:
   hallmark `TC-84KZQM3P`, title `Letter to my daughter`, sealed `1 January 2026`, opens
   `1 January 2036`. Never real user data, never plausible-looking invented letter body
   text presented as a real letter (`04 §12.12`).
3. **No cursors, no hover states, no tooltips, no notification badges.** A screenshot
   with a cursor in it is a demo; without one it is an object.
4. **`tabular-nums` verified in the capture.** If digits are not holding their columns,
   the capture is a defect, not a screenshot.
5. **Register honesty.** The shipped instrument register now defaults to **light paper**
   (`tokens.css` — the T-22 polish pass), with dark as the user's override. Both are real
   product surfaces. Use **dark** for the instrument screenshots below, because the
   register distinction is the identity's central idea and it only reads with dark
   chrome — and say so if asked. Do not present dark as the only surface.

### Device frames

One frame, used everywhere. Consistency across a grid is worth more than variety within
it.

- **Desktop:** a plain browser frame — a single hairline `rgba(22,24,26,.14)` border, a
  6px radius matching the card radius, **no traffic-light dots, no URL bar, no tab
  strip.** Browser chrome is somebody else's identity in the middle of ours.
- **Mobile:** a bare device outline at 10px radius (the sheet radius), no notch drawn,
  no status bar, no home indicator, no glossy screen reflection, no floating perspective.
- **Frame sits on** the paper ground `#E9EAE6` with generous margin — 8% of the frame
  width minimum on all sides. Never on a gradient, never on a coloured brand shape,
  never tilted, never in a 3D perspective mock-up.
- **Contact shadow only** where a physical scene is being described. Flat device frames
  get no shadow at all — instruments don't hover (`04 §7`).

## Part 6 · Motion

**Rules.** Only motions that already exist in the shipped system. No motion-graphics
vocabulary is introduced. Specifically:

- **The mark never animates.** No logo sting, no logo reveal, no logo loader, no logo at
  the end card. The end card is type on paper, held still.
- **Nothing springs, bounces or overshoots.** Ever.
- **Constant velocity for anything representing time.** Never ease out — except at
  transit, where arrival is the point.
- **Registers never crossfade.** Clear → empty frame → arrive.
- **Silent.** No music, no sound design, no whoosh, no ticking. `07 §VII`: *there is no
  sound; that is the sound design.* Captions burned in only where a platform's silent
  autoplay makes the piece illegible — and then set in Source Serif 4, never in the
  platform's default caption style.
- **No kinetic typography.** Type does not fly, letter-space, mask-wipe or type itself
  on. Type appears, or it does not.

| ID | Platform | Length | Motion | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`M01`** | Reel · X | 6s | The held beat — approach at constant velocity, 400ms dead stop, instant state change | The real seal ceremony, screen-recorded. Ends on `Sealed` and holds 2s. Post 05. |
| **`M02`** | Reel | 12s | The decade scrub, driven at constant rate | The real waiting screen scrubbed 2026 → 2036. Dual Clock counts, temperature ramp warms, marker approaches the meridian. Stops one step short of transit and holds. |

### On CTAs

Five of thirteen posts have no call to action at all. That is the design.

Permitted CTA forms, in ascending order of ask:
1. **None.** The default.
2. **A statement of where something is.** *"The full system is published at …"*
3. **An instruction about their own life, not about the product.** *"Choose the date
   first."*
4. **`Keep me informed`.** Used once, on the last post of the month.

an institution's) · `Don't miss` · `Limited spots` · `DM us` · `Tag someone who…` ·
`What would you write? Comment below` · any question posed to drive engagement.

> **Engagement policy.** Replies are answered factually, in the product voice, in the

### 8.1 Open Graph image

> **Flag:** `plan/PROGRESS.md` records this as a genuine open gap from T-21 — *"OG cards
> correct"* was left unmet, and **no Open Graph implementation exists, static or
> dynamic.** This section is therefore a spec for work that has not been done, not a
> description of something shipping. It should be raised as a ticket.

Governing constraint (`04 §12.12`): **no capsule content in OG images, ever** — not the
title, not the recipient, not an excerpt, not the sender's name. An OG image is rendered
by third-party servers and cached indefinitely by parties with no relationship to the
user. `11 §2.3` already settled the approach: **static, per capsule type.** Hold that;
do not build dynamic per-capsule OG generation, however tempting the shareability

## Part 9 · Brand photography direction

### The reconciliation, stated first

`04 §9` forbids photography of people and forbids illustrating the brand's own
metaphors. That leaves a narrow, and better, lane:

> **Photograph only what TimelyCapsule actually makes, and the surface it rests on.**

Printed exports, keepsake cards, recovery kits, embossed plates, the letter on paper.
Nothing that stands in for the product; nothing that stands in for a person. If an object
in frame is not something the product produces, it does not belong in frame.

This is not a limitation dressed as a principle. It is the reason the photography will
look unlike every competitor's, all of whom will shoot a hand holding an envelope.

### Lighting

- **One source.** Soft, large, neutral **5000K**, from the upper left, at a **low raking
  angle** — 15–25° above the plane of the subject.
- The raking angle is the whole technique: it is the only way a **blind deboss** reads at
  all, and the deboss is how the mark appears in physical form.
- **No fill.** Shadows go where they go. A fill card flattens the strike and the strike is
  the point.
- **No rim light, no kicker, no practical in frame, no coloured gel, no window with
  visible blinds, no dappled leaf shadow.** Dappled light is the single most overused
  device in premium product photography and it will date the images by 2029.
- Shadows are **soft, single, and directional**. A double shadow means two sources and
  reads as carelessness.

### Paper

- **Uncoated, always.** Coated stock is glossy and gloss is the opposite of this brand.
- **Grey-green, never cream.** `04 §3` is explicit — the distance from cream is
  deliberate and load-bearing, and drifting warm reintroduces the exact cliché the palette
  exists to avoid. Match to `#E9EAE6` under a 5000K reference; do not white-balance warm
  in post.
- Visible tooth and fibre at macro scale. A slight deckle at one edge is permitted; a
  torn or distressed edge is not — distress is theatre.
- **Heavy.** 300gsm and up. The cut edge is a legitimate subject in its own right, and a
  thin sheet has no edge to photograph.
- Print method: **letterpress or blind deboss.** Never inkjet, never laser, never foil,
  never spot UV, never a gold seal.

### Desk

- **Bare.** One surface, one object, nothing else.
- Permitted surfaces: unfinished pale wood with straight grain · a flat grey-green or
  iron-blue painted plane · more paper. That is the complete list.
- **Forbidden:** marble (it is 2019's luxury signal and it will read as such forever) ·
  concrete · linen or textile backgrounds · leather · a desk mat · anything with a
  visible brand · anything reflective.
- The desk is **not a background.** It is bounded in frame the same way a record is
  bounded on a screen — the same rule, transferred to physical space.

### Objects

Complete permitted inventory. Adding to it requires the same justification as adding an
icon (`04 §5`):

1. A printed letter export.
2. A keepsake card bearing a duration stamp.
3. A recovery-kit record.
4. A struck metal plate bearing a hallmark.
5. A blind-debossed sheet bearing the mark.
6. A stack of the above.

**Forbidden absolutely:** hands, people, any part of a body · pens, ink bottles,
typewriters · envelopes, stamps, wax, string, twine · watches, clocks, hourglasses ·
coffee, plants, spectacles, notebooks, keys · anything that narrates a person's life,
because that person is the user's to imagine and any one we supply displaces theirs.

### Camera and composition

- 100mm macro, **f/8**, tripod, tethered.
- **Dead perpendicular** — flat overhead or straight-on elevation. The only permitted
  deviation is a single fixed 15° rake used consistently across a set when the deboss
  requires it, never mixed with square shots in the same set.
- The subject occupies a **minority of the frame.** Empty surface is the luxury signal and
  it is free.
- **No motion blur, no shallow focus, no hero angle, no "hand entering frame", no
  flat-lay arrangement of multiple props at pleasing angles.** Everything is square to
  everything else.
- Deliver as 16-bit TIFF, no LUT, no film emulation, no grain added in post beyond the
  house's barely-perceptible amount.

### Mood — and how to check it

The target: **an object in a national collection, photographed for the catalogue, by
someone who was not trying to sell it.**

Three tests, applied in review:

1. **The catalogue test.** Would this image look correct beside an accession number and a
   date, with no caption? If it needs a caption to work, it is an advertisement.
2. **The 2035 test.** Would this image embarrass us in 2035? Marble, dappled light, warm
   film emulation and shallow focus all fail this. (`04 §12.15`)
3. **The Kept test.** Does the image show something being *looked after* rather than
   something being *offered*? Offering is the posture of sales. Custody is the posture of
   this brand.

## Part 10 · Production checklist

### Production note — read before generating anything

**Image models cannot set this brand's type, and must not be asked to.**

Every asset that carries a letterform is produced in two stages:

1. **Stage 1 — the plate.** Generate the ground, the paper, the light, the material, the
   deboss, the metal. Prompt explicitly excludes all text. This is where `P##` prompts
   are used.
2. **Stage 2 — the strike.** Set the type in vector — Source Serif 4, IBM Plex Sans, IBM
   Plex Mono, the real files, outlined — and composite it onto the plate with a deboss or
   letterpress treatment. Set the mark from `design/brand/*.svg`, never generated.

**The mark is never generated, at any size, for any purpose.** The 2.1:1
segment-to-notch ratio will not survive a diffusion model, and `brand/IDENTITY.md §1`
states that ratio must survive any future adjustment. Vector only.

**Before any generated asset ships, run the palette check:** sample the output and
confirm no pixel falls outside the declared token set beyond neutral shading. Diffusion
models introduce warm casts, and a warm cast turns this palette into the cream cliché the
whole system was built to avoid.
